# Blood Synergy iOS — Wellness / Paid Content

**For:** Client | **Date:** November 2026

## Why Wellness was removed

Apple rejected the app (Guideline 3.1.1) because Wellness linked to an **external paid video/store** without using **Apple In-App Purchase**.

On iOS, digital content (videos, seminars, courses, unlocks) sold or unlocked in the app **must** use Apple IAP. Linking out to your website/store to buy that content is not allowed.

We removed the Wellness tab and seminar links so the app could be approved. Core features stayed free.

## Your options

**A — Free Wellness**  
Restore the tab with educational content only. No buy buttons, no external store. Fastest and safest.

**B — Paid videos / seminars in the app**  
Must use **Apple In-App Purchase**. Apple takes about 15–30%. No Stripe/PayPal/website checkout for digital content inside the app.

**C — Physical / real-world only**  
Shipped products or clinic bookings can use other payment. Digital videos still cannot.

**D — Leave Wellness off iOS**  
Keep the current approved setup.

*Note: This applies to Apple/iOS. Android rules are different.*

## What we recommend

- Want the tab back soon → choose **A**.
- Want to sell videos/seminars in the app → choose **B**.
- Putting the old external paid store link back will likely get rejected again.
