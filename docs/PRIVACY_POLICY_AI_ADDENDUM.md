## Suggested AI / Gemini paragraph (add under Section 3)

```
The educational assistant uses Google’s Gemini API. After identifying information is removed, we transmit lab values you enter or upload (including images of lab reports), your sex (when provided, because reference ranges can differ by sex), and your chat messages to Google for processing so Gemini can generate educational output.

Google processes this data under its Gemini API terms to provide the service to Blood Synergy and is required to protect submitted data with safeguards appropriate to that service (equal protection relative to how we process it for the same purpose). Under Google’s current Gemini API / Google Cloud terms for API customers, prompts and responses are not used by Google to train or improve Google’s general AI models. Confirm your Google Cloud / Gemini plan settings match this before publishing.

Session lab values and chat content are not retained by Blood Synergy after your session ends. Google’s retention of API request logs (if any) is governed by Google’s then-current API terms and your Google Cloud project configuration. De-identified, aggregated statistics may be retained by Blood Synergy indefinitely to improve the Services (see Section 5).
```
